What we offer

Open play

Drop in, get matched by skill level and rotate through games. Paddles and balls available to borrow.

Coaching and clinics

Group clinics and one-on-one lessons covering serves, dinks, third-shot drops and court positioning.

Leagues and tournaments

Seasonal leagues and one-day tournaments with divisions by level, so every match is competitive.

Private bookings and group events

Book the court for team building, birthdays or a game with friends. We handle equipment and referees.
